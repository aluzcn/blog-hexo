<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>因Docker仓库域名被墙，群晖访问Docker的注册表会出现注册表访问失败。本方法通过代理软件使其能代理局域网其他设备来解决该问题。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4884,"width":"513px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><a href=" /images/2024/04/image.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-4884 size-full" style="width: 513px; height: auto;" src=" /images/2024/04/image.png" alt="" width="847" height="605" /></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>开启Clash的Allow LAN 和System Proxy。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark class="has-inline-color has-vivid-red-color" style="background-color: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0);"><em><s>Windows clash端准备</s></em></mark><br />2.cmd中使用ipconfig查看自己电脑局域网的ip。</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":4886,"width":"529px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"full"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><a href=" /images/2024/04/image-1.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-4886 size-full" style="width: 529px; height: auto;" src=" /images/2024/04/image-1.png" alt="" width="1119" height="511" /></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><mark class="has-inline-color has-vivid-red-color" style="background-color: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0);"><em><s>查看电脑局域网ip</s></em></mark><br />3.群晖端设置代理。勾选通过代理服务器连接，地址填写自己电脑的局域网ip，端口填写clash默认端口7890，点击应用。</p>
